A retail leader who builds for people first.

Riyas V. A. leads the Atlas–Maharani Group and its flagship, Maharani Wedding Collections, Kerala’s largest bridal showroom: 65,000 square feet of premium bridal silk and wedding fashion in Thodupuzha.

His leadership style combines the discipline of modern retail with a deep understanding of Kerala’s fashion, wedding and family-shopping market.

Beyond business growth, his approach places equal importance on team culture, customer trust, employee recognition and community-oriented initiatives.

How he leads

  • Customer first

    Every format in the group starts from what the customer needs on the day: a wedding, a festival, an ordinary Saturday.

  • People before scale

    Growth is only useful if the team grows with it. Recognition, training and culture are run as seriously as sales.

  • Trust is the brand

    Fair pricing, consistent service and keeping promises are what bring a family back for the next occasion.

  • Rooted in Kerala

    The business is built for Kerala's market and gives back to it, from employment to community initiatives.

Strong businesses begin with strong personal foundations.

For Riyas V. A., family represents balance, perspective and continuity. Away from the pace of business, it is the space that keeps priorities clear, decisions grounded and success connected to responsibility.

The same values reflected in his leadership, respect, commitment, consistency and care for people, are also central to the way he approaches life beyond work.

Success becomes meaningful when professional growth and personal responsibility move together.

Six retail brands, one operating standard.

Businesses developed across fashion, family retail, wedding shopping and lifestyle, sharing the same customer-first foundation.

  • Maharani Wedding Collections

    Kerala’s largest bridal showroom: 65,000 square feet of premium bridal silk, sarees, lehengas, gowns, groom and family fashion under one roof in Thodupuzha.

  • Atlas Fashion

Six chapters, in the order they happened.

  1. 1

    A customer-first retail vision

    The journey began with a clear ambition: create a fashion retail experience built on trust, accessibility, strong service and long-term customer relationships.

  2. 2

    Maharani becomes Kerala’s largest bridal showroom

    Maharani Wedding Collections grew into a 65,000-square-foot destination for premium bridal silk, bringing bridal, groom, sarees, lehengas, gowns and family fashion under one roof.

  3. 3

    Twenty-five shops across Kerala

    The business portfolio expanded through multiple retail concepts and locations, creating a wider customer network and a stronger presence across the state.

  4. 4

    A workforce of more than four hundred

    Growth has been supported by a large and diverse team across retail, operations, marketing, management and customer service, turning scale into a people-led organisation.

  5. 5

    A family of fashion and retail brands

    The group now spans distinct retail formats and brands, each designed for a different customer need while sharing the same operational foundation.

  6. 6

    Industry recognition and a world record

    Awards, media recognition and a Guinness World Records title have become part of a larger story of leadership, retail innovation and sustained brand growth.

Frequently asked.

Who is Riyas V. A.?

Riyas V. A. is an entrepreneur from Thodupuzha, Kerala, and the Chairman & Managing Director of the Atlas–Maharani Group, a family of fashion, wedding and lifestyle retail brands.

Which brands are part of the Atlas–Maharani Group?

Maharani Wedding Collections (Kerala’s largest bridal showroom, 65,000 sq ft in Thodupuzha), Atlas Fashion, @less Fashion, @less GO, Megamart and Liza Fashions: 25 shops across Kerala with a team of more than 400 people.

What awards has Riyas V. A. received?

Recognitions include the Times Business Awards Kerala 2026 (Spirit of Excellence), News18 Kerala’s Malayali of the Year, a Guinness World Records title for the world’s largest saree pookkalam, and Businessman of the Year.
