2. Short biography

About 60 words. For event programmes and captions.

Riyas V. A. is the Chairman and Managing Director of the Atlas–Maharani Group, a fashion, wedding and lifestyle retail group based in Thodupuzha, Kerala. Its flagship, Maharani Wedding Collections in Thodupuzha, is Kerala’s largest bridal showroom at 65,000 square feet, specialising in premium bridal silk. The group runs six retail brands across 25 shops in Kerala with a team of more than 400 people. His recognitions include the Times Business Awards Kerala 2026, News18 Kerala's Malayali of the Year and a Guinness World Records title.

3. Full biography

About 200 words. For profiles and speaker introductions.

Riyas V. A. is an entrepreneur from Thodupuzha, in Kerala's Idukki district, and the Chairman and Managing Director of the Atlas–Maharani Group. He leads a family of six retail brands: Maharani Wedding Collections, Atlas Fashion, @less Fashion, @less GO, Megamart and Liza Fashions. Together they operate 25 shops across Kerala and employ more than 400 people in retail, operations, marketing, management and customer service.

The journey began with a customer-first retail vision: a fashion shopping experience built on trust, accessibility and long-term relationships. Under his leadership, Maharani Wedding Collections grew into Kerala’s largest bridal showroom, 65,000 square feet of premium bridal silk and wedding fashion, bringing bridal, groom, sarees, lehengas, gowns and family fashion under one brand. The group then expanded into distinct retail formats, each designed for a different customer need while sharing the same operating foundation.

His leadership combines the discipline of modern retail with a deep understanding of Kerala's fashion, wedding and family-shopping market, and places equal weight on team culture, employee recognition and community initiatives.

He has been recognised with the Times Business Awards Kerala 2026 (Spirit of Excellence, for Maharani Wedding Collections), News18 Kerala's Malayali of the Year, a Guinness World Records title for the world's largest saree pookkalam, and Businessman of the Year.
