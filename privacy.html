Privacy & accessibility.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This website is a personal, informational site. It is designed to collect as little data as possible: no advertising trackers, no analytics cookies, and no third-party content unless you choose to load it.

1. What we collect

Browsing this site does not require an account, a form, or any personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising, fingerprinting, or social-media tracking pixels.

The only personal data we may receive is what you send us yourself: for example, when you email contact@riyasva.com (see section 5).

2. Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies of its own. It does not use local storage or session storage to identify you. Because nothing non-essential is stored on your device, no cookie banner is required under the EU ePrivacy rules, the UK PECR, or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Third-party platforms may set cookies only after you choose to play an embedded video: see the next section.

3. Embedded videos (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram)

The “Media & press” section shows videos hosted by YouTube (Google), Facebook and Instagram (Meta). To protect your privacy, these players are not loaded when the page opens. You see a preview card with a play button; the third-party player is only requested from that platform when you press play. Until then, no connection is made to Google or Meta and no third-party cookies are set.

When you press play, the platform you chose will receive standard technical data (such as your IP address and browser details) and may set cookies according to its own policy. YouTube is loaded in its “privacy-enhanced” mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which does not store viewing data unless you interact with the video. Each card also offers a direct link to watch the video on the platform’s own website instead.

  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram) privacy policy

4. Hosting and server logs

The site is served over HTTPS by a content-delivery network. Like almost every web host, the provider may keep short-lived technical logs (IP address, requested page, browser type, timestamp) for security, abuse prevention and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or profile visitors. The legal basis for this processing, where one is required, is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Fonts are self-hosted on this domain, so no font requests are made to Google Fonts or any other third party.

5. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we will use your name, email address and message only to respond to you. Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any follow-up, and are not shared with third parties except where required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the EU/UK GDPR, India’s DPDP Act, 2023, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or similar laws: including the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because this site holds almost no personal data, the simplest way to exercise any of these rights is to email contact@riyasva.com. We will respond within 30 days.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals by design: there is nothing to opt out of.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone under 18.

8. Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site. It is built to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA: semantic HTML with landmarks and headings, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, minimum 4.5:1 text contrast, 44 px touch targets, descriptive alternative text for every image, carousels that work with the keyboard and screen readers, and full support for the “reduce motion” system setting.

If you find any part of the site difficult to use, please email contact@riyasva.com and we will fix it.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this page when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
